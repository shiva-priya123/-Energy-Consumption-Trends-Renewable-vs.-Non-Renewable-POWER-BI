# 🌍 Energy Consumption Trends: Renewable vs. Non-Renewable Energy

## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores global energy consumption trends by comparing renewable and non-renewable energy sources using **Microsoft Power BI**. The interactive dashboard is designed to help users explore energy usage patterns across countries or regions and understand how energy consumption changes over time.

Through data visualization and comparative analysis, this project supports a better understanding of renewable energy adoption, reliance on conventional energy sources, and broader sustainability trends.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze energy consumption trends over time.
- Compare renewable and non-renewable energy usage.
- Explore energy consumption across countries or regions.
- Visualize the distribution and relative contribution of different energy sources.
- Identify patterns that can support data-driven discussions about energy sustainability.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboard development and data visualization
- **Power Query** — Data preparation and transformation, where used
- **DAX** — Measures and calculations, where implemented
- **Data Visualization** — Trend analysis, comparisons, and KPI reporting

## 📊 Dashboard Highlights

### 1. Global and Regional Analysis
Explore energy consumption across countries or regions to compare energy usage patterns.

### 2. Renewable vs. Non-Renewable Comparison
Compare renewable and non-renewable energy consumption to examine differences in energy-source utilization.

### 3. Time-Series Trend Analysis
Use line and area charts to explore how energy consumption changes across the available reporting period.

### 4. Energy Distribution
Use pie or donut charts, where included, to illustrate the distribution of energy consumption among energy categories.

### 5. KPI and Growth Analysis
Use KPI cards and year-over-year measures, where available, to summarize key indicators and changes in energy consumption.

## 📈 Analytical Questions

This project is designed to explore questions such as:

- How has energy consumption changed over the available time period?
- How does renewable energy consumption compare with non-renewable energy consumption?
- Which countries or regions have higher energy consumption?
- What patterns emerge in the adoption or use of renewable energy?
- How can energy consumption data help inform sustainability discussions?

## 🚀 How to Explore the Dashboard

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Install Microsoft Power BI Desktop.
3. Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop.
4. If Power BI requests access to the original data source, reconnect it to the appropriate dataset.
5. Explore the report pages, filters, charts, and other interactive elements available in the dashboard.
